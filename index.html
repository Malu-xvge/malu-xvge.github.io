The studio

ALUM is run by Malumbo Msopa from Lilongwe. It exists to make the kind of software that is usually skipped: small, local, cheap, and usable without a good connection.

The first release is GameNight, six party games built around Malawian proverbs, trivia and dares. More apps are in the works, and every one of them will be downloadable straight from this site.
