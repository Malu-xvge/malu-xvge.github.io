Privacy policy

Applies to the GameNight Android app and this website. Last updated 12 November 2026.

GameNight has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains in plain language what the app stores, and the one situation in which anything leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a small amount of data in local storage on your device:

  • your points and which categories you have unlocked
  • your settings — sound, music, and the two timers
  • which trivia questions you have already seen, so rounds don't repeat
  • whether you have finished the intro

None of this is uploaded anywhere. It is only on your device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

What is sent when you buy an unlock

Buying something is the only time the app talks to the internet. When you tap a price:

  • The app sends the item you selected and a random payment reference to our payment server. It does not send your name, your phone number or any device identifier.
  • Our server passes the payment request to PayChangu, the Malawian payment processor that handles the mobile money charge. PayChangu collects the mobile money number you enter on their checkout page.
  • We never receive or store your mobile money number. PayChangu processes it, and we only ever see whether the payment succeeded and how much it was for.
  • When a payment is confirmed, our server records the payment reference and amount in its logs so we can verify the payment and investigate complaints. These logs do not contain your phone number.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-up, email address or password.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet — only to start and confirm a payment.
  • Vibrate — for small haptic taps when you answer.

That is the full list.

This website

The site has no cookies, no analytics and no embedded trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the payment server runs on Render; both keep standard web server logs such as IP address, time and requested page for security and troubleshooting, as any web host does.

Downloading an app from this site

Downloading an APK does not require an account or an email address. Your browser and the host may record the request like any other file download. Each download lists its size and SHA-256 checksum so you can confirm the file matches the one we published.

Who processes data for us

  • PayChangu — processes mobile money payments and holds your payment details.
  • Render — hosts the payment server.
  • GitHub — hosts this website and the app downloads.

Children

GameNight is made for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. If you want us to remove a payment record from our logs, email us with the date and amount of the payment and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's update notes.
